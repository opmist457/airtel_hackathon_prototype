Slide 8 of 10 — Go-to-Market
Role of Field Force & Partners
Digital doesn't replace field sales — it supercharges them. Udyam Digital creates a seamless digital+physical hybrid model.
📱

Field Force Enablement

Sales reps use the same platform on their tablets during MSME visits. Walk the customer through advisor, build quotes together, and complete KYC on the spot. No paperwork to carry back to the office.

📊

Back-Office Lead View

Managers see all digital leads with stage, drop-off point, and propensity score. Prioritize follow-ups on high-intent leads that started digitally but didn't complete.

🤝

Channel Partner Portal

Partners get branded access to the platform. Track their referred leads, see commission calculations, and co-browse with their MSME customers.

🎯 Propensity Scoring

Every lead gets a propensity score based on: business segment, engagement depth (how far they progressed), time spent, products viewed. Field force focuses on highest-propensity leads first.

📈 Lead Intelligence

When field reps follow up on digital leads, they already know: what the MSME needs, what they were quoted, where they dropped off, and what bundle to pitch. No cold calling — warm, informed conversations.

✅ Outcome

Digital handles the volume. Field force handles the value. Together, they convert more leads at lower cost with better customer experience.
